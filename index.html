How Suggest Outfit Works

Suggest Outfit scores every top/bottom pairing in your closet and favors the combinations stylists consider flattering, instead of picking at random.

  • Neutrals go with almost anything. Black, white, grey, navy, brown, and beige/tan are versatile enough to pair with nearly any other color in your closet.
  • Analogous colors work well together. Colors that sit close together on the color wheel (like navy and teal, or red and orange) read as intentional rather than accidental.
  • Complementary colors make a bold, put-together look. Colors from opposite sides of the wheel (like red and green, or blue and orange) balance each other out.
